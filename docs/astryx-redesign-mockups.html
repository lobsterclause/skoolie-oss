Teachers & contacts

The three actions a parent takes most are full-width buttons at the top. Teachers are grouped once per person, tinted by their course, with a 44px mail target. Harvested teacher links (family.teacherLinks: website, Classroom, Schoology, syllabus) sit on their own line under the course — never in the end slot, where they collided with Email on a phone. No link on file → a muted "Find site" search. Tapping the name opens a teacher sheet with every link and where it was found.
